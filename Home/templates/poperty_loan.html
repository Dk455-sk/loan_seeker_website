What is Loan Against Property?

Loan Against Property (LAP) is a secured loan where your residential or commercial property is used as collateral. This unlocks the value of your assets while you continue to own them.

You can get up to 80% of your property value depending on your income, eligibility, and bank policy. This makes LAP ideal for large personal or business expenses.

How to Apply

  1. Submit Application: Provide basic details and intent to apply.
  2. Document Collection: ID, address proof, income docs, property papers.
  3. Verification: Lender verifies eligibility and does property valuation.
  4. Sanction & Disbursement: Loan approved and amount transferred.
