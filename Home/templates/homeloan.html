What is a Home Loan?

A home loan is a secured loan offered by banks or financial institutions for the purchase, construction, or renovation of a residential or commercial property.

  • Loan amount up to 80–90% of the property value
  • Flexible repayment tenure (up to 30 years)
  • Lower interest rates compared to other loans

Eligibility Criteria

  • Age between 21 to 65 years
  • Minimum income threshold
  • Stable employment or business
  • Good CIBIL/credit score (preferably 750+)

Documents Required

For Salaried
  • ID & Address Proof
  • Last 3 months salary slips
  • 6 months bank statement
  • Form 16
For Self-Employed
  • Business registration proof
  • ITR of last 3 years
  • Bank statements
Common
  • Passport-size photos
  • Loan application form
  • Processing fee cheque

Tips Before Applying

  • Compare rates from different banks
  • Check EMI using a calculator
  • Choose suitable repayment tenure
  • Review processing fees & hidden charges
