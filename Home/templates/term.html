TERM LOANS

A term loan is a monetary loan that is repaid in regular payments over a set period of time. Term loans usually last between one and ten years, but may last as long as 30 years in some cases. A term loan usually involves an unfixed interest rate that will add additional balance to be repaid.

Types of Term Loan

  • Short-term Loan: Repaid within 12 months.
  • Long-term Loan: Repaid over longer periods, typically 5 years or more.

Term Loan Category

  • Secured Loan: Requires collateral such as equipment, property, etc.
  • Unsecured Loan: No collateral required but higher interest rates.

Term Loan Examples related to Business Loan

  • Working Capital Loan: Short-term funds to manage cash flow, repaid within 12 months.
  • Overdraft: Credit limit with interest only on used amount, tenure 1-12 months.
  • Equipment Financing: Financing for equipment or vehicles, loan amount varies widely.

Pros and Cons of Term Loan or Term Financing

Pros
  • Lower Interest Rates
  • Higher Loan Amount
  • Loan approval within 59 minutes
  • Longer repayment tenure
  • Quick Loan amount disbursal
  • No Collateral required for Unsecured Business Loans
  • Interest paid is tax deductible
Cons
  • Long-term burden of loan & EMIs on borrower
  • High CIBIL/credit score required
  • Guarantor is required
  • High business volume and vintage
  • Good business stability with profitability
  • Collateral required – Term loans are mostly secured
  • Non-repayment of loan/EMI results in high penalty
