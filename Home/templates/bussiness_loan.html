Why Business Loans Matter

Financial flow is the lifeblood of any successful organization. Whether you're starting up or scaling up, timely financing is critical. At The Loan Wale, we provide the resources you need to take your business to new heights.

Types of Business Loans

  • Unsecured Business Loans: Term loans for up to 3 years for eligible firms with a sound financial background.
  • Secured Business Loans: Working capital funding in INR or foreign currency with collateral.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Age: Between 24 to 65 years
  • Current Business: Minimum 3 years with profits and stable billing
  • Business Experience: At least 5 years in relevant industry

Documents Required

Loan Tenure & Prepayment
