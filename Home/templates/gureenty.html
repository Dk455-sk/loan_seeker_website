Meaning of Bank Guarantee

A bank guarantee is a kind of guarantee from a lending organization. It signifies that the bank ensures that the liabilities of a debtor are going to be met. If the debtor fails to perform the obligation, the bank will cover it. This allows the customer to acquire goods, purchase equipment, or draw down a loan.

Parties Involved
  • Applicant: Requests the guarantee and borrows from a creditor.
  • Beneficiary: Receives the guarantee.
  • Bank: Issues the guarantee and pays if the applicant defaults.
Types of Bank Guarantees
  1. Guarantee of credit return
  2. Guarantees of advance payment return
  3. Guarantee of payment
  4. Contract execution guarantee
  5. Tender guarantees
  6. Guarantee in favor of customs authorities
  7. Guarantees of warranty execution
