What is a Personal Loan?

A personal loan is a flexible loan that you can use for anything — your child’s wedding, dream vacation, medical expenses, or even shopping. No collateral or security is needed. Loan amounts typically range from ₹50,000 to ₹30 Lakhs with tenures of 1 to 5 years. The process is usually quick and hassle-free.

Banks may charge two main fees: a processing fee (2–3% of the loan amount) at the time of applying, and a prepayment fee (2–3%) if you close the loan early. Both fees are negotiable depending on your profile.

Personal Loan Eligibility

Banks prefer applicants with stable income to reduce risk. Eligible applicants include:

  • Salaried individuals
  • Self-employed professionals
  • Self-employed business owners

How Much Loan Can You Get?

Your loan amount is based on your repayment ability. Typically, EMIs for all your loans combined shouldn't exceed 40% of your net monthly income.

Personal Loan Interest Rates

Interest rates vary between 14% to 26% depending on your credit score, income, and chosen lender. Check out current offers across banks for the best match to your profile.

How Fast Can You Get a Personal Loan?

Banks generally disburse loans within 7 working days. Keep all documents, including Post Dated Cheques (PDCs), ready to avoid delays. Speed depends heavily on the quality of your documentation.

Add a Co-Applicant

You can apply with a co-applicant to increase your eligibility. The combined income is considered while evaluating the loan amount. Ideal co-applicants include your spouse, parent, or sibling.
